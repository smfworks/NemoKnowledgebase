# Rubric — mischief variant board

Frozen 2026-10-31 before any paid video call. Do not add or move an item after a clip is opened.

## What a point means

Each scored clip is watched from extracted frames (0.0s, 1.0s, 2.0s, 3.0s, 4.0s, last). Score only the four items below.

| Item | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| motion_asked | The named action is not in the clip | Part of the action, or a weaker version of it | The named action is clearly there |
| identity | The subject or room is replaced | Drifted, but still recognizable | Same subject and layout as the still |
| artifacts | Morph, flicker, or geometry that makes it unusable | Noticeable, still watchable | Clean enough to pin on a board |
| board_keep | Discard | Maybe, if nothing better is on the board | Pin this tile |

`board_keep` is the call for the X pack. A 2 is a post. A 1 can be included and labeled as a maybe. A 0 stays in the lab folder.

## What is not a score

- Company speed and quality claims versus Wan 2.2
- Whether the clip has audio (`generate_audio` is false on the endpoint)
- Taste, beyond the four items
- A single 720p reroll versus its 480p twin. Report both. Do not crown one.
