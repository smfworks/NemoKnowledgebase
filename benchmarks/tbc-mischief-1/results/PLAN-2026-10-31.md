After the board is scored, re-roll keepers (`board_keep` >= 1) at 720p, seed 42, duration 5. Those files are the X pack. Do not treat one 720p clip as a quality winner over 480p.

Dollar cap: $1.00 of summed `usage.cost`. Stop before the next paid call if the next call would cross it.

## Metrics (frozen here)

Computed, from files:

- HTTP status and error body on probes
- Wall seconds from submit to `completed`
- `usage.cost` on the poll body
- ffprobe width, height, duration, fps, codec, audio streams, bytes
- First-frame lock: ffmpeg SSIM of the input still, scaled to the output size, against extracted frame 0. Also SSIM of frame 0 against the frame at 2.0s and the last frame, as drift.

Watched rubric, 0/1/2, scored only after the frames are opened. Do not add items after seeing the clips.

- motion_asked: 0 none, 1 partial, 2 the named action is clearly there
- identity: 0 replaced, 1 drifted but recognizable, 2 same subject
- artifacts: 0 unusable, 1 noticeable but watchable, 2 clean enough to pin
- board_keep: 0 discard, 1 maybe, 2 pin it

Judge: Nemo. The model under test does not score itself. No comparison model in this suite, so no winner language.
